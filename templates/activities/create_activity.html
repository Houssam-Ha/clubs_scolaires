💡 Tips for Creating Great Activities

  • Be Descriptive: Write a clear, engaging description that explains what participants will do and learn.
  • Set Realistic Capacity: Consider the space and resources available when setting participant limits.
  • Choose the Right Type: Select the activity type that best matches your event for better discoverability.
  • Include Requirements: List any materials, skills, or preparation needed in advance.
  • Add an Image: Visual content makes activities more appealing and gets more registrations.

📋 Activity Types Guide

📋 Meeting: Regular club meetings, discussions, planning sessions
🔧 Workshop: Skill-building sessions, tutorials, hands-on learning
🎉 Event: Celebrations, shows, exhibitions, special occasions
🏆 Competition: Contests, tournaments, competitive activities
💪 Training: Skill development, practice sessions, coaching
🤝 Social: Social gatherings, networking, team building
🌍 Community: Service projects, volunteer work, outreach
🚌 Trip: Field trips, excursions, educational visits

⚠️ Important Notes
